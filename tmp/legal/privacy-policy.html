LEGAL // PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy
Policy

This policy explains what Cognautic collects, what it does not collect, and how Forge and related products handle user data.

01 // OVERVIEW

Built To
Minimize
Data Collection

Cognautic does not sell personal data. We do not operate Forge as a data-harvesting service, and we aim to keep user-controlled workflows local wherever practical.

01

Data We Do Not Intend To Collect

We do not intentionally collect or sell the contents of your files, prompts, chats, credentials, API keys, browser sessions, or Google Workspace content beyond what is required to provide a feature you explicitly invoke.

02

Telemetry

We may collect limited product telemetry, diagnostics, crash information, feature usage, install metadata, and similar technical signals to improve reliability, performance, usability, and security.

03

User-Provided Integrations

If you connect external providers or services such as LLM APIs, Google Workspace, browsers, or MCP tools, those services may process your data under their own terms and privacy policies.

02 // DETAILS

How Data
Is Used

01

Local Operation

Forge is designed around local execution. Local files, shell output, browser state, and conversation context may remain on your device unless you choose a feature that sends them to a provider or remote service.

02

Authentication Data

Where authentication is required, tokens may be stored locally on your device in order to keep integrations working. Those tokens are used only to perform actions you authorize.

03

Telemetry Purpose

Telemetry, where enabled, may be used to understand failures, prioritize fixes, measure feature adoption, and improve product quality. It is not sold as user data inventory.

03 // RIGHTS

Your
Choices

01

Your Control

You choose which providers, tools, integrations, browsers, and execution modes to enable. You are responsible for the data you submit to third-party services through Forge.

02

Third-Party Services

OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, OpenRouter, Groq, Cerebras, browser vendors, MCP servers, and other external services are independent controllers or processors of data they receive from your use of Forge.

03

Contact

For privacy questions, contact cognautic@gmail.com.
